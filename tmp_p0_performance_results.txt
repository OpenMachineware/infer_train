## Analysis

The optimization successfully improves decode performance by **28.9%**, bringing it from 76.34 t/s to 98.38 t/s. This confirms that matching llama.cpp's threadgroup configuration (NSG=2) significantly improves GPU utilization.

### Why it works
1. **Better GPU utilization**: 2 simdgroups per threadgroup reduces threadgroup launch overhead
2. **Improved memory coalescing**: Adjacent simdgroups can share memory access patterns
3. **Reduced scheduling overhead**: Fewer threadgroups to schedule (m/2 vs m)
